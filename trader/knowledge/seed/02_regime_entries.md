# Regimes, entries and what works where

## regime
- **Identify the regime first**: Before any entry decide whether the market is trending (ADX above about 22, EMAs stacked and price on one side of them) or ranging (ADX low, price oscillating around a flat average). Trend tools lose in ranges and reversion tools lose in trends.
- **Higher timeframe agrees**: Trade in the direction of the trend one timeframe up (for 1h entries, the 4h or daily). Counter-trend trades against a strong higher-timeframe trend need a much better reason and a smaller size.
- **Volatility expansion means stand aside or size down**: When the ATR as a percentage of price jumps above its recent 90th percentile, stops get hit by noise. Halve size or wait for volatility to normalise.
- **Trends persist longer than they should**: Do not short something only because it has gone up a lot, and do not buy something only because it is cheap relative to last week. Wait for structure to change (a lower high after an uptrend, a higher low after a downtrend).
- **Ranges end with a breakout on volume**: A close outside a multi-week range on volume at least 1.5x the average is the start of a trend more often than a trap; a breakout on weak volume that closes back inside the range is a trap more often than a trend.

## entry
- **Pullback in a trend**: In an uptrend, buy the first pullback to the 20-EMA that holds and closes back above it with RSI no longer overbought (below 60). Mirror for shorts in downtrends. This is the highest-quality repeatable entry in a trending market.
- **Breakout confirmation**: Enter a breakout on the close beyond the level, not on the touch. Intrabar pokes through highs are where stops get hunted.
- **Mean reversion only inside a range**: Fade moves to the edge of a range when RSI is at an extreme (below 30 or above 70) AND price closes back inside the Bollinger band. Never fade a move in a trending regime.
- **Confluence before commitment**: Require at least two independent reasons that agree (regime plus a rules signal plus a level or a candle). One indicator alone is a coin flip.
- **Do not chase extended moves**: If price is more than 2 ATR away from the 20-EMA, the move is extended; wait for a pullback rather than buying the top of the bar.
- **Avoid the first minutes after major news**: Spreads widen and price whips. In forex skip 15 minutes around scheduled high-impact releases; in crypto skip the first candle after an exchange-wide liquidation cascade.
- **Volume confirms**: An entry in the direction of above-average volume is worth more than the same setup on thin volume. Volume below 50% of its 20-bar average means the market is not participating.

## exit
- **Trend change closes trend trades**: A long from a trend setup is closed when price closes below the 50-EMA, regardless of the P&L.
- **Failed breakout reversal**: If a breakout entry closes back inside the range within two bars, exit immediately; failed breakouts often run hard the other way.
